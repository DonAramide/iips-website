Invify by IIPS

Smart School Management, Right in Your Pocket

Invify is a smart school and business management app for Nigerian schools and retail businesses. Manage student records, invoicing, fee collection, report cards, inventory, and receipts from your phone, even offline.

Download on Play Store Choose Your Edition

Single app download. Mode switches based on setup (School Edition or Retail Edition).

Real screens from the Android app — analytics, billing, and results in one place.

Features

Everything needed to run school and retail operations efficiently from one mobile app.

Student Management

Admissions, profiles, class assignment, payment tracking.

Fee & Invoice Management

Generate bills, track payments, and part-payments with balance tracking.

Results & Report Cards

Enter scores, compute averages, and print professional report cards.

POS Thermal Printing

Instantly print receipts via Bluetooth thermal printers.

Academic Calendar

Manage terms, sessions, promotions, and progression history.

Teacher & Class Management

Assign teachers, map classes, and track classroom setup.

Retail Mode

Full invoicing and stock operations for businesses, not just schools.

PIN-Protected Admin Access

Protect settings and sensitive operations from unauthorized changes.

Works Offline

Continue operations without internet and sync when available.

FAQ

Do I need internet to use Invify?

No. Invify is designed to work offline fully.

Can I migrate data between devices?

Yes, migration support can be provided through backup/restore workflows.

How do activation codes work?

Each edition is licensed using activation codes with one-time payment options.

Does it support thermal printing?

Yes. Invify supports Bluetooth thermal receipt printing.
